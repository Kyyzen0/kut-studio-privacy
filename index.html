Kut-Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · App covered: Kut-Studio, a video editor for macOS (Mac App Store version)

In short

Kut-Studio is a video editor that works entirely on your Mac. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no audience-measurement tool, no tracking and no built-in third-party service.

Who publishes Kut-Studio?

Kut-Studio is published by Audryk Chesse. For any question about this policy: audryk.chesse@icloud.com.

What Kut-Studio stores on your Mac

To work, Kut-Studio keeps information on your Mac only, in the places listed below. The sandbox of macOS restricts the app to its own private space and to the files you choose.

  • Your projects (.kut files): saved where you choose. A project describes your edit (clips, effects, titles) and the paths of your media; the media themselves stay where they are.
  • Recovery copies: while you work, Kut-Studio writes an automatic backup next to your project (.kut.autosave) so you can recover your work after a crash.
  • Voice-overs: a recording made in the app is saved as an audio file in an enregistrements folder next to your project.
  • Your files and exports: the videos you export are written where you choose.
  • Preferences (language, theme, interface, performance settings): a small settings file in the app's private space.
  • Caches (previews, thumbnails, waveforms, analysis results, hardware detection): temporary files in the app's private space, which you can delete at any time.
  • A diagnostic log (kut-studio.log): a text file that records technical errors of the app (error messages and the part of the program involved, with the date), so a problem can be understood. It can mention the name of a file being processed. It stays on your Mac; Kut-Studio never sends it. You only share it if you choose to copy it and send it yourself.

None of this information is sent anywhere. The publisher has no access to it.

Data collected or transmitted

None. Kut-Studio has no account system, no server of its own and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tool. It does not send your projects, your media, your exports or your usage anywhere. Editing, previewing, exporting and analysing your videos all happen on your Mac.

Permissions requested

  • Files you choose. Kut-Studio can only open and write files and folders that you select yourself (through the open and save dialogs, or by opening a project). It does not browse your disk on its own.
  • Microphone, only if you record a voice-over. macOS asks your permission the first time. The microphone is used only while you record; the sound is saved as a file on your Mac and is never sent anywhere. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone; everything else keeps working.

Kut-Studio does not use your location, contacts, calendar, photos library, camera or screen recording.

Purchases and free trial

Kut-Studio offers a free trial period. Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The publisher never receives your card, your payment details or your Apple identity; the app only reads from the system whether you currently have the right to use the features (trial, subscription or purchase). Apple processes purchase data under Apple's privacy policy. Apple may give the publisher aggregated sales and download statistics that do not identify you.

Third-party services

Kut-Studio includes no third-party service for advertising, analytics, tracking, social networks or crash reporting. It uses open-source libraries (such as Qt and FFmpeg) that run on your Mac to display the interface and to read and write video and audio; they do not communicate with any server.

Updates

Updates of the Mac App Store version are delivered by the Mac App Store, under your system settings. The app itself does not look for updates and contacts no server for that purpose.

Children

Kut-Studio is a general-purpose video editor and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect any personal data, whatever the user's age.

How long is data kept? How can I delete it?

Everything stays on your Mac for as long as you keep it. You can:

  • delete your projects, recovery copies, recordings and exports like any other file, in the Finder;
  • delete the caches from the app's preferences, or by deleting the app;
  • delete the app's remaining data (preferences, caches, log): uninstall Kut-Studio, then remove its folders in your user Library (~/Library/Containers) if you wish.

Your rights

Since the publisher holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on their side (including under the GDPR). You stay in control of your data: it is on your Mac and you can erase it at any time as described above. For purchase data, see Apple's policy above. For any question, write to the contact address given above.

Changes to this policy

If Kut-Studio changes in a way that affects the above (for example by adding an account, an online service or a data-collecting tool), this policy will be updated before the new version is released, and the date at the top of the page will be changed.
